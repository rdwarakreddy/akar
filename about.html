01 / Manifesto

Who We Are

Based in South Bengaluru, AKAR Design Studio is an established architecture and interior design practice built by a dedicated team of creative architects, skilled interior designers, and experienced site engineers. We believe that building a home or office should be an exciting, well-organized journey rather than a stressful task.

Over the years, our studio has partnered with numerous families and businesses to deliver custom residential villas, modern apartments, and functional commercial interiors. We take time to understand how you live or work, ensuring every layout makes sense for your daily routine.

From drawing board sketches to final civil execution and custom furniture setup, our integrated team works closely together to maintain strict quality standards, transparent budgets, and timely handover.

02 / Approach

Our Design Philosophy

We believe great design is not just about aesthetics — it’s about creating experiences. Every space we design is carefully planned to maximize functionality, comfort, natural flow, and timeless appeal.
