FAQs

Questions, answered

What does turnkey mean at AKAR?

add

Turnkey means AKAR manages architectural design, structural engineering, interior decoration, material selection, and site supervision together under one roof, connecting you with an expert residential architect and home designer team.

How is turnkey different from design-only services?

add

In a design-only engagement, you receive floor plans, house interior design drawings, and architectural specifications to hire separate contractors. In a turnkey engagement, our commercial architecture firm and interior design specialists build and supervise the project directly.

How much does flat and home interior design cost?

add

Pricing depends on room dimensions, selected material grades, kitchen and wardrobe customization, and overall architectural scope. We provide itemized, transparent quotes after initial consultations.

How long does a typical project take?

add

Apartment and flat interior design projects typically range from 6 to 12 weeks, while full villa sustainable building design and turnkey execution vary based on structural complexity.

What services do your interior designers provide?

add

Our professional interior designers offer living room interior design, bedroom interior design, kitchen interior design, wardrobe interior design, and hall interior design solutions.
